Token launch radar

Find the token
before it exists.

Sonar-9 scans 758 protocols and ranks the ones with no token on record by what their token would be worth — from the protocol's own TVL, its seven-day growth and how long it has run with no token, through a curve fitted to the 95 protocols whose tokens already trade and tested out of sample. Each row also carries token odds and expected value, plus a farm plan sized at the minimum viable deposit: what to do, what it costs, how long it takes — and the net expected value at the low end of the range. The page checks itself: the cover carries the self-check, and the model section carries the split-conformal range with measured blind coverage, the keep/drop table and the placebo controls.

How to read this board

01

Implied value

What the token would be worth if the record stayed as it is. A model, not a price.

02

Pre-token, kept apart

Only protocols with no token on record. The ones whose token trades sit in their own view, with their real market cap.

03

Farm plan on every row

Open any protocol for seven concrete steps, sized at the minimum viable deposit — the deposit threshold, where the low-end net is zero — with the verdict at $50, $100 and the threshold, the costs itemised per outflow, and the plan window dated. The verdict follows the low end, rated A, B, C or Skip.

How implied value is computed

One function scores every protocol. No name is special-cased, and no row is hand-picked. The page recomputes each row and says whether it matches.

The fitted curve, drawn

Tested out of sample

Data: the sealed DefiLlama protocol snapshot, fetched 2026-11-14. The watchlist is the floor — 40 protocols at or above $50M of TVL with no token on record — never the ceiling: the early radar adds 158 more. The ledger accounts for all 758.

Implied value is D Sonar's claim, not a prediction of whether or when a token is issued, and not financial advice.
