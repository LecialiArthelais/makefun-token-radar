Find the token
before it exists.

D Sonar scans 758 protocols and ranks the ones with no token on record by what their token would be worth — from the protocol's own TVL and its seven-day growth, through a curve fitted to the protocols whose tokens already trade and tested out of sample. Every row carries a farm plan: what to do, what it costs, how long it takes — and the net expected value.

How to read this board

01

Implied value

What the token would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is. A model, not a price — and never a promise that a token launches.

02

Pre-token, kept apart

The board holds only protocols with no token on record. The ones whose token already trades sit in their own view, with their real market cap.

03

Farm plan on every row

Open any protocol for seven concrete steps: the action, the capital, the gas, and the hold — and the net expected value, rated A, B, C or Skip. No slogans, no paid claims, no blind approvals.

How implied value is computed

One function scores every protocol. No name is special-cased, and no row is hand-picked. The page recomputes each row from the same figures and says whether it matches the board.

Tested out of sample

The curve against live protocols

Data: the sealed protocol snapshot, DefiLlama protocol index, fetched 2026-11-14. The watchlist is the floor — 40 protocols at or above $50M of TVL with no token on record — never the ceiling: the early radar adds 138 more below it.

Implied value is D Sonar's claim, not a prediction of whether or when a token is issued, and not financial advice. A protocol on this board may still have a token its record does not carry.
